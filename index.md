            <p>The High-Performance Human Interfacing Laboratory (Hi-PHI Lab) at UNC develops transformative <b>human interfacing</b> technologies to enable a new era of human <b>health</b>.</p>
            <p>The world is flooded with wearable technologies, but none of those sensors precisely, robustly tell you what is happening inside your nerves and muscles. In the Hi-PHI Lab, we're building those needed sensors. </p>
            <p>We design devices that can sense, stimulate, and image the human body using novel electromagnetic strategies. Our vision is a world where accessible, minimally-invasive technologies transform lives by restoring ability to persons with movement disorders.</p>
